Linggen Health

Apple Health shows the same app to everyone. Linggen Health composes the app from you: the agent works out who you are from your data, builds the screen for that person, plans your next days, and changes as you change. Every change says why. One tap undoes it.

Pick a person. The phone below is the same code, one catalog of cards, three different apps. It works on the phone alone; a Mac is optional and adds the months and the work side. Every figure is example data.

First run · confirm, then one question

  • Within the first minute the profile pass has read the last months. Yinyue opens with what she found and one tap confirms it. Nothing the data already holds is asked.
  • Then the goal, the one field data can never infer. Plan waits for it and says so.
  • Later asks come only when a decision needs them, one at a time, with the consequence.

Behind it

  • profile.json from HealthKit characteristics + workouts + the latest weigh-in; name from Yinyue's first meeting in memory; goal written with source: user.

Home · composed, with the why on top

  • The layout is written by the weekly pass from the profile. The why-line says what moved and why; Undo restores last week's layout and pins it for four weeks.
  • Numbers change every day. The shape changes only weekly or when you act.
  • ⋯ on a card: Pin, Hide, Why this. Your choice wins forever.

Behind it

  • Profile → compose → layout.json (cards, order, headline, why, previous), run on the phone; one widget per catalog kind; mirrored to a paired Mac as a last-writer-wins register.

Plan · the next seven days

  • Written Sunday from goal, level, load, adherence and the calendar. Outdoor athletes get the weather joined per day; indoor ones never trigger the weather call.
  • The morning pass may move one day, and says why in gold.
  • The plan lives here. Calendar is a mirror, later, opt-in.

Behind it

  • plans/<week>.json + targets.json on the phone; Open-Meteo only when profile.athlete.environment is outdoor. A paired Mac takes the weekly pass over the long store.

Track · checklist, then the chart the goal needs

  • Items come from the plan and targets. HealthKit checks what it can see (a workout, a weigh-in, protein from a food app); you tap or say the rest.
  • Progress is the goal's own chart: weight against the goal line, per-km time, active minutes. Then heart history and adherence.

Behind it

  • The checklist derives from the plan and targets on the phone; progress from progress/<metric>.json. A check is a local write, synced to a Mac when there is one.

Workouts · the Watch in full

  • HR series at 5 s, splits, zones from your measured max, route, recovery, effort, power and cadence.
  • "vs your last five" of the same type, then one paragraph on what to do next.

Patterns · the kill risk, handled in code

  • Forming until 4 aligned weeks and one MAD of effect; stable after 6; retired after two contradictions.
  • Every claim carries its evidence rows.

Who you are · the profile, visible

  • Inferred from years of workouts, the scale, your routine, what you open, what you said. Never a quiz.
  • Each row shows confidence and evidence. "Not quite" corrects it, and the correction wins over inference.

Behind it

  • profile.json: body from HealthKit characteristics + the latest weigh-in; athlete from workouts (indoor flag, routes); routine from sleep + Apple Shifu; goal from what you said.

Data · show everything

  • Every type with count, last sample and sources. Apple hides read denials, so an empty type says both possible reasons instead of 0.

Settings · the user is never gated

  • Layout changes are automatic with why and undo; the history is here. Hidden cards stay hidden. The plan lives in the app. Weather only for outdoor sessions. Your Mac is optional.

Rules

What keeps it honest

The layout moves weekly, or when you act

Never daily. Every change has a why and a previous. Undo pins last week's layout for four weeks.

The profile shows its evidence

A field appears only above 0.6 confidence. Below that, Home asks one question instead of guessing. Your correction wins.

Targets show their formula

2.0 g/kg × 78.4 kg. Change a body fact and the target re-derives on screen.

No brand unprompted

Supplements as evidence in plain words. A product is named only when you ask, from a live search, with the source.

A pattern needs weeks; a weight trend needs two

Forming until 4 aligned weeks and one MAD. Weight is a 7-day average; one weigh-in is never a headline.

Personal baseline beside every number

28-day median and spread. Never a population range, never Apple's colours.

Empty is not zero

Apple hides read denials. An empty type shows both possible reasons. An auto-checked item names its source.

Works alone

The phone is the whole product. A Mac adds the years and the work side. A Mac alone never pretends to see the body.

Never a questionnaire

Confirm what the data holds, ask the one thing it cannot know, ask later only when a decision needs it.

Wellness only, yours only

No diagnosis, no medication advice. Data stays on your phone and your Mac; never iCloud, never sold, never for ads.
