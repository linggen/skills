Brief · the front door

  • One sentence, at most four evidence chips, at most one action. Written by the morning-brief mission the moment last night's sleep lands.
  • Chips are doors: tap Sleep and you are on the Sleep screen.
  • The action is proposed in v1; Do it is one tool call and shows as a chip in Yinyue's thread.

Behind it

  • GetSleep · GetDay · GetLife · GetPatterns → WriteBrief → retained health/brief
  • Memory recall for goals and last week's context.

Today · every number beside your normal

  • 28-day median and spread per metric. Never Apple's colours, never a population range.
  • The Work tile is the join, visible every day even when nothing is wrong.

Behind it

  • daily/<date>.json ⋈ life/<date>.json; baselines from baselines.json.

Workouts · the Watch in full

  • HR series at 5 s, splits, zones from your measured max, route, recovery, effort, running power and cadence.
  • "vs your last five" of the same type, then one paragraph on what to do next.

Behind it

  • Watch ends the workout → background delivery wakes the phone → outbox → next connect drains → GetWorkout → workout report mission.

Sleep · stages, debt, what tracks with it

  • Stages from the Watch, overnight HRV, wrist temperature and breathing rate against your normal.
  • "What tracks with it" shows the evidence rows and says when three nights are only a hunch.

Behind it

  • GetSleep + FindPattern(sleep × commits_after_23).

Patterns · the kill risk, handled in code

  • Forming until 4 aligned weeks and an effect of one MAD; stable after 6; retired after two contradictions.
  • Every claim carries its evidence rows. Grey cards show what is forming and how many weeks are left.

Behind it

  • Nightly patterns pass → patterns.json. Stable ones go to memory in your words at the weekly report.

Data · show everything

  • Every type with count, last sample and sources. Tap one to read raw rows.
  • Apple hides read denials, so an empty type says both possible reasons instead of showing 0.

Behind it

  • GetLedger over state.json; raw rows from samples/<type>/.

Settings · the user is never gated

  • Which types, brief time, nudges and quiet hours, how blunt, calendar policy, sync state per Mac, export.
  • Calendar writes stay propose-only until the confirm tier exists.

Rules

What keeps it honest

Personal baseline beside every number

28-day median and spread. Never a population range, never Apple's colours.

A pattern needs weeks

Forming until 4 aligned weeks and one MAD of effect. Stable after 6. Retired after two contradictions. Evidence rows always attached.

Empty is not zero

Apple hides read denials. An empty type shows both possible reasons.

Wellness only

No diagnosis, no medication advice. Medications are context the agent reads and never raises.

Local

Phone → Mac over WebRTC. Never iCloud, never sold, never for ads. Cloud model sees a day's summary only with consent.

One writer per mutation

Brief, patterns, notes, goals each have one script. Page buttons and agent tools call the same one.
