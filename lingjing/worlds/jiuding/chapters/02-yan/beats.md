# Chapter 2 · 兖州之鼎 — beat sheet

The second open chapter, in chapter 1's shape: six scenes at four places
of 兖, the spine as waypoints; the province's seeds, market and creatures
fill the days between. Opens 2026-11-01 for everyone; its gate is 2, so
the cauldron takes one at the peak of 筑基 into 结丹.

1. **Arrive** (`02-arrive`, 濮水). South from 冀 the road flattens at the
   Pu. Clear water, mulberries along the bank, and thunder from the east
   under a cloudless sky. An old man fishing will not turn round — two
   envoys waited three days to make him a minister, and he chose the mud.
   Asked about the thunder he lifts his rod: east, follow it.
2. **Puyang** (`02-town`, 濮阳). The notice: forty days of thunder over
   雷泽, fish floating in 大野, no ferryman will sail; the magistrate
   offers a reward to whoever stops it. The market inside the Mulberry
   Gate (the place's shelf; Trade). Yinyue: the cauldron woke under the
   thunder, and it is beating — like a heart.
3. **The lake** (`02-lake`, 雷泽). White water. In the middle stands 雷神,
   dragon-bodied, man-headed, beating its own belly; every beat is a crack
   of thunder. Not anger — urgency: the cauldron woke beneath it and its
   drum cannot stop. Three ways: the fight (五行, its root wood), its
   riddle (雨落田上 — 雷), or 舜's way — yield the shore, sit, and wait,
   as the people of 雷泽 once gave way to one another. Any of the three
   quiets it. The water parts.
4. **The deeps** (`02-deep`, 雷泽深处). The cauldron in the mud, sealed by
   a slab cut with the 河图 — the east left blank. Which numbers belong
   there (三八). Answer → the cauldron.
5. **The cauldron** (`02-cauldron`, 雷泽深处). 兖鼎 rises; the thunder
   stops. At the peak of 筑基 the Core forms (the breakthrough exit; not
   yet at the peak, the cauldron waits — *筑基未满*). Yinyue's second
   memory: the hand that laid her in the water had a cauldron's pattern
   on its back, and it counted to nine.
6. **East** (`02-end`, 雷泽深处 → 濮阳). The fish sink back, the ferries
   sail. The third cauldron's breath lies east, in 青, between the sea and
   Mount Tai. The chapter ends.

**Never in chapter 2:** what the cauldrons are for, whose hand it was, any
realm above 结丹, the names of the other seven cauldrons.
