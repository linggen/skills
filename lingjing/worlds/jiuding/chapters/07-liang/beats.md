# Chapter 7 · 梁州之鼎 — beat sheet

The seventh open chapter, in the shape of 1–6: six scenes at four places of
梁, the spine as waypoints. No lock while the game is built (`opens:
null`); gate 7 — the cauldron takes one at the peak of 合体 into 大乘. 梁 is
new (places/liang.json): in from 荆's 巫峡 by 江州, on over the Qinling to
雍 by the 褒斜 plank road.

1. **Arrive** (`07-arrive`, 岷江). The 江 comes down red; the fields of 蜀
   lie under water; a stone rhino on the bank (《华阳国志·蜀志》：外作石犀五头以
   厌水精) and the stone man of 李冰's pact — 水竭不至足，盛不没肩 — the water
   over its shoulders.
2. **Chengdu** (`07-town`, 成都). The notice: the weir at 离堆 is cracking; at
   night a great ox fights the river. The market (蜀锦; 《禹贡》梁州：厥贡璆、
   铁、银、镂) — Yinyue: *原来铃是这里的银。* (alone: the bell rings faintly).
   An old man tells 李冰's fight (《风俗通义》：南向腰中正白者，我绶也).
3. **Lidui** (`07-weir`, 离堆). 夔牛, an ox of a thousand jin, butts the weir:
   the cauldron woke under the mountain and it thinks 李冰 is back (《中次九
   经》：岷山……多夔牛; 郭璞注：重数千斤; 《史记·河渠书》：蜀守冰凿离碓). Three
   ways: the fight (root earth; an elite), its riddle (午字出了头 — 牛; or
   深淘滩，低作 — 堰), or 李冰's way — *深淘滩，低作堰* (the six-character rule
   cut at Dujiangyan, held to be his): dig the bed, lower the weir; the water
   finds its way, the ox lies down.
4. **The deeps** (`07-deep`, 石纽 · 洗儿池). The pool where the infant Yu was
   washed (《蜀王本纪》：禹本汶山郡广柔县人也，生于石纽; 《史记·六国年表》：禹兴于
   西羌). The seal: 禹步 cut in the stone — three steps, the prints hidden:
   how many? (《抱朴子·仙药》：如此三步……后有九迹 — 九; 《尸子》: Yu's lameness
   was the pace).
5. **The cauldron** (`07-cauldron`, 石纽). 梁鼎, cast with 岷, 嶓, 沱, 潜 and a
   bear (《禹贡》：岷嶓既艺，沱潜既道……熊罴狐狸织皮). At the peak of 合体 the
   Mahayana (大乘; not yet — *合体未满*). An old shepherd calls Yu *文命*
   (《史记·夏本纪》：夏禹，名曰文命). Yinyue's seventh memory: *就是这个名字。她叫
   的不是我——是一个会走遍九州的人。* (alone: the old man names the name.)
6. **North** (`07-end`). The 江 runs clear; the stone man's shoulders show.
   The eighth breath lies over the 秦岭 by the plank road, in 雍 — where the
   cauldrons were carried when Zhou fell.

Also in 梁: 窃脂 on 岷山 (《中次九经》：状如鸮而赤身白首……可以御火; plate
《古今图书集成·禽虫典》竊脂圖), 蜀锦 at 成都's market, twenty seeds
(seeds/liang.json). 夔牛's picture is newly drawn — no labelled plate found.

**Never in chapter 7:** her secret said aloud, what happens when the nine
are gathered, any realm above 大乘.
