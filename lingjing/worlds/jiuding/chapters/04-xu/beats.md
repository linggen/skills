# Chapter 4 · 徐州之鼎 — beat sheet

The fourth open chapter, in chapters 1–3's shape: six scenes at four places
of 徐, the spine as waypoints; the province's seeds, market and creatures
fill the days between. No lock while the game is built (`opens: null`);
its gate is 4, so the cauldron takes one at the peak of 元婴 into 化神.
Yinyue walks it (she joined at 结丹); every line of hers has its `alone`.

1. **Arrive** (`04-arrive`, 泗水岸). Back where the player woke. The reeds
   are cut; a thousand ropes lie along the bank; divers' fires. The water
   sinks. The divers: a chain down there, thick as a beam.
2. **Pengcheng** (`04-town`, 彭城). The notice at the South Gate: the
   governor will raise the Zhou cauldron from the Si as the First Emperor
   tried (《史记·秦始皇本纪》：始皇还，过彭城，斋戒祷祠，欲出周鼎泗水。使千人没水
   求之，弗得); three divers lost at 吕梁. The market (the place's shelf; the
   sounding stones and pearls of 《禹贡》 徐州：泗滨浮磬，淮夷蠙珠暨鱼). Yinyue:
   the rope broke halfway — something below is biting (the Han pictorial
   stones' 泗水升鼎, where the rope is bitten through as the cauldron rises).
3. **The mouth** (`04-mouth`, 泗口). The ropes have fouled the chain at the
   foot of 龟山, and 无支祁 wakes (《古岳渎经》, in 《太平广记》卷四百六十七：形若
   猿猴，缩鼻高额，青躯白首，金目雪牙，颈伸百尺……颈锁大索，鼻穿金铃，徙淮阴之
   龟山之足下). Three ways: the fight (五行, its root metal; an elite), its
   riddle (四条水 — 泗; or where Yu chained it — 龟山), or the 吕梁 swimmer's
   way (《庄子·达生》：与齐俱入，与汩偕出，从水之道而不为私焉) — go down with the
   whirl, cut the ropes, never the chain. It sleeps.
4. **The deeps** (`04-deep`, 泗渊, under 彭城). A rope-scar on the
   cauldron's ear. The seal: King Wen's chart (后天八卦 — 离南、震东、兑西),
   Zhou's own; the north blank (坎).
5. **The cauldron** (`04-cauldron`, 泗渊). 徐鼎 rises with no rope — cast
   with 淮, 泗, 峄阳孤桐 and 泗滨浮磬. A silver trace of a bell rises with it.
   At the peak of 元婴 the spirit is transformed (化神; not yet — *元婴未满*).
   Yinyue's fourth memory: *那铃是陪着这口鼎的。我摇过它——我那时有手。*
   Then half a sentence at the water — *水上少了一个* — the secret begins
   (alone: the moon on the water trembles, as if one reflection had gone).
6. **South** (`04-end`). The notice comes down; the divers go home. The
   fifth breath lies across the Huai, in 扬, where Yu gathered the lords.

**Never in chapter 4:** whose hand it was, the name it spoke, what happens
when the nine are gathered, her secret said aloud, any realm above 化神.
