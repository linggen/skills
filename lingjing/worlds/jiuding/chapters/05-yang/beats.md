# Chapter 5 · 扬州之鼎 — beat sheet

The fifth open chapter, in the shape of 1–4: six scenes at four places of
扬, the spine as waypoints; seeds, market and creatures between. No lock
while the game is built (`opens: null`); gate 5 — the cauldron takes one at
the peak of 化神 into 炼虚. 扬 is new: its places (places/yang.json) come in
from 徐's Huai ferry by 广陵 (《左传·哀公九年》：吴城邗，沟通江淮) and go on
up the 江 by 彭蠡.

1. **Arrive** (`05-arrive`, 震泽). 《禹贡》：三江既入，震泽底定 — but the lake
   has risen three years and the three rivers run backward. A fisherwoman
   picks great bones out of the shallows.
2. **Gusu** (`05-town`, 姑苏). The notice at 阊门: every night a giant walks
   toward 会稽 and never arrives; each step lifts the water. The market (《禹贡》
   扬州：厥贡惟金三品……篠簜……厥包橘柚). Yinyue: he is going to the assembly.
3. **Kuaiji** (`05-hill`, 会稽山). 防风氏, bones like cart-axles, still walking
   to the gathering he came late to (《国语·鲁语下》：吴伐越，堕会稽，获骨焉，节
   专车……仲尼曰：昔禹致群神于会稽之山，防风氏后至，禹杀而戮之; 《史记·夏本纪》：
   禹会诸侯江南……因葬焉，命曰会稽). Three ways: the fight (root wood; an
   elite), his riddle (人在云上 — 会; or whose bone — 防风氏), or the
   roll-call — *防风氏，到* — he has arrived, and sits down. Yinyue: *晚到也是
   到了。* (alone: under the moon, late still counts as arrived.)
4. **The deeps** (`05-deep`, 禹穴). The cave 司马迁 climbed (《史记·太史公自
   序》：上会稽，探禹穴). The seal: 《尚书·洪范》's five — 一曰水，二曰火，三曰
   木，四曰金，五曰土 — the first blank (水): Yu's work was water.
5. **The cauldron** (`05-cauldron`, 禹穴). 扬鼎, cast with the three rivers,
   震泽 and orange trees. At the peak of 化神 the Void is refined (炼虚; not
   yet — *化神未满*). An old woman's face on its belly, a cauldron's pattern
   on her hand. Yinyue's fifth memory: *她不是我娘。我没有人，她也没有人，就凑
   成了两个。* (alone: the face looks at you as if trying to know someone.)
6. **West** (`05-end`). The rivers run to the sea; 震泽 falls three feet;
   oranges before Yu's tomb. The sixth breath lies up the 江, in 荆, where
   someone once asked what a cauldron weighs.

Also in 扬: 长右 at 彭蠡 (《南次二经》：其状如禺而四耳……见则郡县大水; plate
《古今图书集成·禽虫典》長右圖), 橘柚 at 姑苏's market, twenty seeds
(seeds/yang.json).

**Never in chapter 5:** the name the hand spoke, the night Qin came, her
secret said aloud, any realm above 炼虚.
