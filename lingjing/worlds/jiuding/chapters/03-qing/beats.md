# Chapter 3 · 青州之鼎 — beat sheet

The third open chapter, in chapters 1 and 2's shape: six scenes at four
places of 青, the spine as waypoints; the province's seeds, market and
creatures fill the days between. No lock while the game is built (`opens:
null`; the launch sets its date); its gate is 3, so the cauldron takes one
at the peak of 结丹 into 元婴.

1. **Arrive** (`03-arrive`, 潍水). East from 兖 the road follows the Wei to
   the sea. A man fishes with a straight hook and no bait (太公's way —
   whoever is willing bites). The tide of the East Sea has not gone out in
   forty-nine days; ask in 临淄.
2. **Linzi** (`03-town`, 临淄). The notice under the 稷门: the tide stands,
   no boat puts out, and every dawn a city stands on the sea off 蓬莱 and
   does not fade. The masters of 稷下 argue why. The market inside the gate
   (the place's shelf; Trade — Qi's salt and silk). Yinyue: the cauldron is
   breathing *in* under the sea; the tide is in its belly.
3. **The shore** (`03-shore`, 蓬莱). The city on the sea is light — the
   light of 夔 (《大荒东经》: ox-shaped, blue-grey, hornless, one-legged; its
   light like sun and moon, its voice like thunder; 黄帝 made a drum of its
   hide). It stands at 流波山 calling: the cauldron's breath under the
   mountain sounds to it like another 夔 answering. Three ways: the fight
   (五行, its root water), its riddle (一日一月 — 明), or 孔子's word —
   *夔一而足*: one of it is enough. Any of the three quiets it; the tide
   draws back.
4. **The deeps** (`03-deep`, 流波山). At low tide the mountain's root shows,
   and under it the cauldron, sealed by a slab cut with the eight trigrams —
   乾 south, 坤 north, 坎 west, the east blank: 伏羲's own chart (先天). Which
   trigram belongs there (离). Answer → the cauldron.
5. **The cauldron** (`03-cauldron`, 流波山). 青鼎 rises, cast with the sea,
   岱山 and a rising sun. At the peak of 结丹 the 元婴 forms (the
   breakthrough exit; not yet at the peak, the cauldron waits — *结丹未满*).
   Yinyue's third memory: the water she was laid in was salt — this sea —
   and the hand said *等九鼎聚齐*, then a name that was not hers.
6. **South** (`03-end`, 流波山 → 蓬莱). The tide turns; the city fades;
   the boats put out. The fourth cauldron's breath lies south, in 徐 — by
   the 泗水, where the player woke. The chapter ends.

**Never in chapter 3:** whose hand it was, the name it spoke, what happens
when the nine are gathered, any realm above 元婴, the names of the other
six cauldrons.
