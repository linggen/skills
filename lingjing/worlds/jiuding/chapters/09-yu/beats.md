# Chapter 9 · 豫州之鼎 · 定鼎 — beat sheet

The last chapter. Six scenes at four places of 豫, the spine as waypoints;
no lock while the game is built (`opens: null`). **No new realm**: 渡劫 is
the ladder's last, so the chapter has no gate (`gate: null`) and its
cauldron is not a breakthrough — it is the ending, 定鼎 (`ending:
{ id: "dingding" }` on chapter.json, for the rules to mark when the chapter
ends). Only the 定鼎 path: no "carry them away" branch. 豫 now has its
places (places/yu.json): in from 雍's 太华山 through 函谷; 定陶 joins 洛邑 by
嵩山.

1. **Arrive** (`09-arrive`, 函谷关). The pass-keeper asks for your book, as
   尹喜 asked 老子 (《史记·老子韩非列传》：关令尹喜曰：子将隐矣，强为我著书);
   purple air from the east (《列仙传》). Yinyue: only one of her left on the
   water (alone: one moon on the eastern waters).
2. **Luoyi** (`09-town`, 洛邑). Nine empty stone footings where the nine
   stood (《左传·宣公三年》：成王定鼎于郏鄏，卜世三十，卜年七百); the 灵脉 thinned
   the day they left. The market — the goods of all eight provinces. A light
   moves on 和山. Yinyue grew up among the footings, ringing the bell.
3. **Heshan** (`09-hill`, 和山). 泰逢 keeps the nine confluences (《中次三经》：
   实惟河之九都……九水出焉，合而北流注于河；吉神泰逢司之，其状如人而虎尾……出
   入有光。泰逢神动天地气也); your eight cauldrons make the nine waters rise.
   It asks what you will do with nine. Three ways: the fight (root earth — the
   centre; an elite whose deck holds all five), its riddle (可以临水 — 河; or
   what tail — 虎尾), or its answer: *在德不在鼎* — set them back, never hold
   them. Yinyue: *她会喜欢你的。我早说过。*
4. **The deeps** (`09-deep`, 洛水). Where the turtle brought the writing
   (《周易·系辞》：洛出书) — and where a baby was found under a moon. The seal:
   the eight cauldrons in a ring, the centre empty → 豫 (中; 德 is taken too).
5. **定鼎** (`09-cauldron`, 洛水). 豫鼎 rises — 伊, 洛, 瀍, 涧 (《禹贡》：伊洛瀍涧
   既入于河). The ninth thunder falls among the nine, not on the player.
   Yinyue whole: *原来只有一轮。* (her own riddle, yinyue-moon: 千江有水千江月;
   alone: the moon on the Luo closes into one). The player carries the nine
   to 洛邑 and sets them on the footings; the 灵脉 run back to every province
   — they keep the breath, not the bronze.
6. **After** (`09-end`, 洛邑). The one button: *问：你留下吗？* — *你问了。那我
   就留下。* (alone: the moon stops still in the middle of the Luo.) The
   provinces stay open; seeds keep coming. The chapter ends (`ends`), with
   no chapter after it.

Also in 豫: 马腹 at 伊阙 (《中次二经》：其状如人面虎身，其音如婴儿，是食人;
plate 《古今图书集成·禽虫典》馬腹圖); 泰逢's plate is the same encyclopaedia's
神異典 太逢神圖. 洛邑's shelf carries the eight provinces' goods; twenty seeds
(seeds/yu.json).

**Never:** a "carry them away" (问鼎) path; any realm past 渡劫.
