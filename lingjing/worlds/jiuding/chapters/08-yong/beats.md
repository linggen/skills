# Chapter 8 · 雍州之鼎 — beat sheet

The eighth open chapter, in the shape of 1–7: six scenes at four places of
雍, the spine as waypoints. No lock while the game is built (`opens:
null`); gate 8 — the cauldron takes one at the peak of 大乘 into 渡劫, the
last realm on the ladder. 雍 is new (places/yong.json): in from 梁 over the
Qinling by the 褒斜 plank road; 太华山 looks east to 函谷 (豫).

1. **Arrive** (`08-arrive`, 渭水). Three years' drought; the 泾 and the 渭
   are both dust — no clear, no muddy (《诗·邶风·谷风》：泾以渭浊). A bronze
   shard in the riverbed, off a cart.
2. **Yongcheng** (`08-town`, 雍城). Qin's old seat. Old men say nine carts
   came in from the east with the cauldrons (《史记·秦本纪》：周民东亡，其器九鼎
   入秦) — and under the cloth the carts were empty. The market (《禹贡》雍州：
   厥贡惟球、琳、琅玕). The rain rite: 《大荒东经》：旱而为应龙之状，乃得大雨.
3. **Taihua** (`08-cliff`, 太华山). 肥遗 on the cliff drinks the cauldron's
   breath off the 渭 (《西山经》：太华之山，削成而四方……有蛇焉，名曰肥遗，六足四
   翼，见则天下大旱). Three ways: the fight (root fire; an elite), its riddle
   (日在干上 — 旱; or what its sight brings — 大旱), or the old rain rite —
   shape a dragon of mud (应龙之状); the rain comes, it flies west.
4. **The deeps** (`08-deep`, 陈仓). Rain fills the pool under the 陈宝 shrine;
   the stone crows at night (《史记·封禅书》：若雄鸡，其声殷云，野鸡夜雊). The
   seal: twelve pitch-pipes, the first missing — 黄钟 (Qin set one measure for
   all: 《史记·秦始皇本纪》：一法度衡石丈尺).
5. **The cauldron** (`08-cauldron`, 陈仓). 雍鼎, cast with 泾, 渭, 弱水, 黑水
   (《禹贡》：黑水西河惟雍州。弱水既西，泾属渭汭). At the peak of 大乘 the first
   thunder: 渡劫 begins (not yet — *大乘未满*). Yinyue remembers the night:
   the keeper counting the nine into the provinces' waters (《史记·周本纪》: Qin
   took the cauldrons); keeping watch was her own asking. Then: *有件事我没说。*
   — the secret: every cauldron raised, one fewer of her on the water; when
   the nine are gathered no water will hold her. (alone: the pool holds no
   moon though the sky has one.)
6. **East** (`08-end`). Rain three days; the 泾 clear, the 渭 muddy again.
   The ninth breath lies east through 函谷, at the centre — the 洛, where the
   nine once stood.

Also in 雍: 羬羊 on 钱来山 (《西山经》：其状如羊而马尾……其脂可以已腊; plate
《古今图书集成·禽虫典》羬羊圖), 琅玕 at 雍城's market, twenty seeds
(seeds/yong.json). 肥遗's plate is the same encyclopaedia's 肥遺圖.

**Never in chapter 8:** what happens when the nine are gathered; any realm
above 渡劫.
