# Chapter 1 · 冀州之鼎 — beat sheet (第十回)

> **Rebuilt 2026-09-30** to the book's 第十回 (his: 「可以，照这样重做冀州」). 筑基 moved to the
> 沉鼎观 cliff in 第九回 — its own chapter, `00-zhuji` (its scenes stand in 徐, and a chapter's
> scenes stand in its province). No 狍鸮 at the altar (it still haunts 冀's places). The chapter's
> fight is **冰夷's two dragons testing the one who would not kneel** (`01-rise`, `foe-shuanglong`),
> written into the book too. Each scene's `story` is the book's own passage (verbatim zh + en).

1. **Arrive** (`01-arrive`, 漳水南岸). 筑基后第十天 the abbot's map; the tenth evening at the Zhang;
   周衡 does not write a word.
2. **Ye** (`01-ye`, 邺城). The inn, the custom, this year's bride (豆腐西施). The two failed plans are
   the player's choices — **bribe the Three Elders** (the ingot's 圈里一横) and **the bedsheet ghost**
   (the white-robed 「仙长」 in the shrine) — then **the eighteenth**.
3. **The wedding** (`01-altar`, 河伯祠). He sends the shaman 「去给河伯报信」; 阿禾 jumps in without
   counting; the Three Elders cough up twenty years of River Lord money.
4. **The Zhang stands up** (`01-rise`, 河伯祠) — **the chapter's fight**: the river stands, twenty
   boats, thousands kneel, three stand. 冰夷's dragons lunge and sweep (a duel with `foe-shuanglong`,
   a trial that may be fought again, `retry`); won, the book's passage plays: he steps back in front
   of 豆腐西施 and stands between the dragons without a spell — 「她是卖豆腐的。她没招谁。」
5. **The deeps** (`01-deep`, 漳渊). 「谁说我要娶媳妇的？」; 阿禾's 「账就烂了」; 「你身上，有青丘的味道」;
   the name 冰夷; 「方才那两条龙，是替她问你的」; the Luo seal (riddle `seal-ji`; 「……足。」, six and
   eight swapped).
6. **The first 鼎** (`01-cauldron`, 漳渊). The cauldron shrinks into his palm, the token's second tail,
   赵昂 under the willow — 「天下，该归于一」 — 银月's first memory (`memory: 1`). **No breakthrough.**
7. **Willow Bend** (`01-end`). 豆浆; 「记账的，总要去对一对」; 周衡's sister. Ends the chapter; the second
   鼎 lies east, in 兖.

**Never in chapter 1:** what the cauldrons are for, 银月's second memory, any realm above 筑基, the
names of the other eight cauldrons.
