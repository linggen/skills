# 第一章 · 外门 — panel shot list

**Status:** all 21 panels in `art/panels/wm-*.webp` are **DRAFTS**, painted
2026-09-29 with the local FLUX recipe (tools/paint-waimen.py) and eye-checked for
fake characters. They are to be repainted by a better model from these
descriptions (Hanli, 2026-09-29: the prologue's FLUX panels had wrong scale,
Western houses, inconsistent characters and seams).

**For every panel.** 连环画 / 小人书 plate: fine ink line (白描) with a light wash
on aged paper, 3:2 landscape, no text, no seals, no signs, no calligraphy. The
setting is a Taoist temple in the hills of 徐州 in an unnamed ancient dynasty:
grey-tiled roofs with upturned eaves, rammed-earth walls, lattice paper windows,
stone-flagged courtyards, oil lamps, no glass, no Western furniture.

**The recurring cast — keep them the same across panels:**

- **The hero** — 13–14, slight, in an outer disciple's plain grey robe a little
  too big, a small wooden fox token on a cord at the neck. Never a face that
  says boy or girl: from behind, small, or turned away. Hair tied up simply.
- **阿禾** — the hero's age, always the other gender, so also never gendered:
  grey robe one size too big with the sleeves rolled three times, a red-tipped
  nose, a little stitched notebook and a brush. From behind or small.
- **银月** (only as a spirit) — a tiny translucent silver fox with ONE tail, a
  faint moonlight glow; sits on the hero's shoulder or knee.
- **马小宝** — 15, plump, round smug face, fine silk robes (brighter than anyone),
  later a round bronze mirror at his waist; five hangers-on in plain robes.
- **周衡** — young steward, thin, stern, a thick ledger always open, a brush behind the ear.
- **褚先生** — lecturer, fifties, thin, a small goat beard, grey scholar's robe.
- **孙二狗** — 15, tall and skinny, patched grey robe, a small hand-sewn cloth charm at his waist.
- **瞿老** — very old gatekeeper, bent, bamboo broom, plain hemp robe, calm face.
- **玄沉子** — the abbot, mild, grey robe, no ornament.
- **内门师姐** — 18, pale blue inner-disciple robe, hair tied high, a plain straight Chinese sword on her back.
