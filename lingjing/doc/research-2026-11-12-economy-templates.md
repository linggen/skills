## 闭关: do they burn currency per session?

**No — not as the main loop.** In both idle xianxia games read, every lasting speed-up is a permanent level or a capped lever:

- **Permanent levels**: the 聚灵阵 (想不想修真: 25,000 灵气 at level 60, 125,000 at 139; the cost doubles past 100; 一念逍遥's
  guides say to level it first). Paid mostly in materials (wood, herbs, stone, map items), not 灵石.
- **Small burns exist**: 想不想修真's 练功房, 1,000 灵石 for 5× cultivation, once a day past a certain realm (the once-a-day
  is from a snippet); 一念逍遥's shop refresh.
- **Daily caps do the rest**: 20 pills and 5 吐纳 a day (一念逍遥); 200 auto-runs, half yield after 100 (想不想修真).
- **Offline accrual**: 想不想修真 "8 hours, 9 with a card" (snippet only); 一念逍遥's rule **unverified**.

So the afternoon's 「聚灵, then 吐纳」 (灵石 burned in each 闭关) is not what the genre does. The genre's shape: free capped
accrual, a permanent 聚灵阵 level as the large sink, a small once-a-day booster.

## What stops a rich player buying it all

1. Hard daily caps on purchases (6 resin refills, 10 shop refreshes, 20 pills).
2. A price ladder inside the cap (50 → 200 原石; the weekly boss 30 then 60).
3. Gates tied to progress, not wealth (the pill cap resets on a breakthrough; prices follow the tier).
4. Falling yield (half after 100 runs; the best claims only three times a week).

The general model: taps and sinks — add a sink with a sharply rising cost for every tap (Machinations).

## Not confirmed

原神: 浓缩树脂's cost (40 or 60), Mora income and sink sizes (one third-party site), no developer statement on why the open
world pays little. 炉石: signature and diamond cards as cosmetic-only. Marvel Snap: today's upgrade prices; that matches
stay fair whatever is spent (one source says Collection Level feeds matchmaking). 一念逍遥: offline accrual, 秘境 entry,
base breakthrough rates, 丹毒. Hades · Slay the Spire: no run gating found.
