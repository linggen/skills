# 修为 gates that strand the player: proposals (2026-10-27)

Hanli's rule (2026-10-27): a gate must never leave the player stranded with little to do. The grind belongs in the content-rich stretch before the gate, so he arrives at 85–95% of it and waits no more than one sitting (~30–40 min of play) at the door. Four gates break that rule after numbers phase 2. This doc proposes fixes. No game data is changed.

## How these numbers were made

- **Base.** `main` @22ea2b3a. Each option was tried in a scratch worktree: the ladder or scene edit first, then `node tools/numbers.mjs --apply` (the master refit, which is what would ship, since tests hold the dry run empty), then the gate rule. A cell reads `typical arrival / straight-through arrival · wait`. "below" means he arrives a layer short.
- **Arrival at a split gate** is of the part asked (40% of a half-layer reads 80%).
- **Content was emulated** with classic boards already in the world (五子棋, 象棋 at 6 min; 拼图, 华容道 at 4 min), played at ★2. "7 boards" is about 36 min of board play. Real boards would be of the 游戏厅's classic kinds, chosen per scene.
- **Baseline:** 柴房 40%/20% · 71/95m · 秘境 100%/88% · 大比前夜 29%/below · 51/82m · 09-yishui 100%/72% · 100m · 09-futian below · 258m · 09-dabi below · 264m.

## Three things that hold for every gate

1. **The refit decides.** `master.lines.gu.hours.fit.gates` protects only `wm-mijing` today. For any other gate, the band fit (typical 5–7.5 days a 回) re-prices the layers and quietly undoes the fix. Example: five boards before 大比前夜 measure 84%/44% before the refit and 56%/20% · 45/82m after it. With `wm-qianye` added to `fit.gates`, the same five boards read 100%/88% · 5m. **Every option below includes adding its gate to `fit.gates`.**
2. **Pay alone cannot fix them.** Control run: every gate in `fit.gates`. The fit cuts the layers to 0.3–0.6 h (a layer in ~25 min). 古七 drops to 3.2 typical days and 古八 to 4.5, both under their bands. Even so, 大比前夜 still reads below · 31m, 09-futian below · 26m, and 09-dabi below · 96m. The gap is content, not price.
3. **The tool undercounts in two places.**
   - It doesn't play 执事堂's east wall (3 杂役 tags a day). Counted at ~10 min a tag, the wall takes ~12 min off every wait (大比前夜 typical 51 → 39m). That is not enough to close any gate, and at 五/六层 the errand share is only ~3%.
   - It measures arrival as he enters the gated scene, so that scene's own boards (大比前夜's 掐诀) never count.

   Also, `09-dabi` is measured only on its 入秋 way (九层). The 腊八 gate (八层) is never measured.

**Placement limits** (`content.mjs lintGate`): a gate may not stand inside a key beat after its entry. That leaves 古四 two spots: `wm-chaifang` (the 柴房 beat's entry) or the 秘境 door. It leaves 古六 `wm-qianye` (the 大比 beat's entry) or `wm-baishi` after it. In 00-zhuji every scene before 瞿老's cliff is open.

**Split gates (b)** are a rule change. `rules/gates.mjs gateMet` and `stageBrief`, `tools/balance.mjs`, and `master.mjs` gatesOk would all have to accept a fraction of the layer.

## 古八 · 09-dabi (腊八 八层圆满 · 入秋 九层圆满)

The book gives three years (年六 · 年七 · 年八) at a layer a year, and the game has one scene for all three. 入秋 must stay 九层圆满: 宁师伯 says 「没到这一步的，筑基丹吃了也是白吃」. Lowering 腊八 alone measures nothing, because both gates sit on one scene.

| Option | After refit | Cost |

| Pay (09-dabi in `fit.gates`) | below · 96m (control) | 七层 through 九层 at 0.5–0.6 h. 古八 lasts 4.5 days |
| (a + c) **Split into the book's years**, a gate each: 年六 (考书 · 看炉 · 神识 · 轻身) asks 七层圆满, 年七 (进退 · 阵课 · 小炉) asks 八层圆满 at 腊八, 入秋 asks 九层圆满. 09-dabi's passages already hold this text. ~7 boards a year; 2 of them (考书, 看炉) exist | 年六 92%/100% · 年七 90%/88% · 9m · 入秋 100%/100% | 七层 through 九层 at 1.0–1.2 h. 古八 lasts 5.0 days. **Recommend (a + c), 7 boards a year.** Nothing in the book changes; its three years get their scenes back. 西墙's red tags (the book's 「执事堂的差事牌一块一块揭，一年一层」) are a natural extra, but they were not counted.

**Built 2026-10-28.** 年六 09-dabi + 09-shenshi (神识 · 轻身, ¶29–45) · 七层圆满; 年七 09-dingfeng (定风 · 阵 · 进退) · 八层圆满, then 09-laba (腊月, the final); 年八 09-yanceng (孙二狗 四层) · 入秋 九层圆满. Twenty boards. After the refit: 大比前夜 100%/88% · 7m, 09-dingfeng 100%/88% · 7m, 年六 and 入秋 ✓; 七–九层 0.89 · 0.97 · 1.06 h; 古八 5.7 days.
