Scenes

What it shows

  • The chat carries the game. Story, choices and free text live in it; the cards are the same on both screens.
  • Ling drives. Narrates the world, gives every task, answers anything typed.
  • Yinyue is at your side. The only 3D figure — the moon above the stream.
  • Tasks earn 修为; 100 fills a layer. The real-life task counts only when Shifu confirms.
  • Typing resolves scenes. At 夫诸, type 喂它灵芝 / feed it the lingzhi — an exit no button offers — or answer its riddle in your own words.
  • 灵气 is tokens, shown as the 丹田. Typing and story choices spend it; 连连看 never does.
  • 四灵根 for everyone in v1 — 木 水 火 土.
  • Mac: the scene on the left, the conversation on the right. Yinyue stands in the current place with the card that matters now; today's tasks sit below; choices are asked in the chat as AskUser. The board grows to 6×6.
  • Phone: the chat is everything — the same cards arrive inline.
  • A creature is never named without its picture.

Drawn, not real

  • Ling reads typed words by keyword; the real game asks a model to map them to the scene's exits.
  • The moon stands in for Yinyue's 3D model.
  • The 斗法 board and the Shifu confirmation are placeholders.
  • 连连看 here matches any pair; the real board uses the path rule.
  • The pictures and the map are sketches; real art comes from the classical illustrated 山海经 or is drawn to match.
