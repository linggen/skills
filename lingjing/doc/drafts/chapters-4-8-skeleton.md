# Chapters 4–9 — beat-sheet skeletons (draft)

*Draft for Hanli to review; not game data. Each chapter is in the shape of
01–03's beats.md: six scenes at four places, the spine as waypoints, the
gate = the realm its cauldron breaks. Yinyue's beats follow
`yinyue-backstory.md`; every line of hers carries `alone`.*

**Order.** 03-qing already points to 徐 (the prologue's province has no
cauldron — its gate is null). From 徐 the road loops: south to 扬, up the 江
to 荆, up the gorges to 梁, over the 秦岭 to 雍, east through 函谷 to 豫 — the
center, where 成王 set the nine (定鼎于郏鄏). product-spec lists the nine in
禹贡 order (…荆 豫 梁 雍); this order differs only so the finale is at 洛.
**Open question:** the ladder ends at 渡劫 (gate 8); chapter 9 needs a
terminal step (飞升) or an ending flag. **Existing place ids:** only 徐's
(sishui, pengcheng, sikou, lvliang…) and 豫's `dingtao`; the rest are proposals.
**Art:** 无支祁 and 防风氏 have no 1597 plate — alternatives given.

## Chapter 4 · 徐州之鼎 (`04-xu`, gate 4 → 化神)

Heritage: 《史记·秦始皇本纪》始皇过彭城，使千人没水求周鼎，弗得 (seed xu-02); the
Han pictorial stones 泗水升鼎 (the rope bitten through as the cauldron rose);
《庄子·达生》吕梁丈夫蹈水; 《古岳渎经》禹锁淮涡水神无支祁于龟山之足. Places:
`sishui` 泗水岸, `pengcheng` 彭城, `sikou` 泗口, new `siyuan` 泗渊 (under 彭城).

1. **Arrive** (`04-arrive`, 泗水岸). Where the player woke. The reeds are cut;
   a thousand ropes lie along the bank; divers' fires. The water pulls *down*.
2. **Pengcheng** (`04-town`). The notice at the South Gate: the governor will
   raise the Zhou cauldron from the Si, as the First Emperor tried; three
   divers lost at 吕梁 this week. Market (泗滨浮磬, 淮夷蠙珠). The breath runs to 泗口.
3. **The mouth** (`04-mouth`, 泗口). The divers' ropes have fouled the chain
   at 龟山's foot; 无支祁 (形若猿猴，青躯白首，金目雪牙), half woken, drags it. Fight
   (root metal); riddle (四条水 — 泗); or the 吕梁 swimmer's way — *与齐俱入，
   与汩偕出*: go down with the whirl, cut the ropes, not the chain. He sleeps.
   (Art alternative: 合窳, 《东次四经》, 见则天下大水.)
4. **The deeps** (`04-deep`, 泗渊). A rope-scar on the cauldron's ear. The
   seal: 文王's chart (后天八卦, Zhou's own), the north blank → 坎.
5. **The cauldron** (`04-cauldron`). 徐鼎 rises with no rope — cast with 淮,
   泗, 峄阳孤桐 and the sounding stones. Peak of 元婴 → 化神 (*元婴未满*).
   Yinyue: *那铃是陪着这口鼎的。我摇过它——我那时有手。*
6. **South** (`04-end`). The notice comes down. The fifth breath lies across
   the Huai, in 扬, where Yu gathered the lords.

**Never:** whose hand, the name, the nine gathered, her secret, above 化神.

## Chapter 5 · 扬州之鼎 (`05-yang`, gate 5 → 炼虚)

Heritage: 《国语·鲁语下》吴伐越，堕会稽，获骨焉，节专车——仲尼曰：禹致群神于会稽之山，
防风氏后至，禹杀而戮之; 《史记·夏本纪》禹会诸侯江南，因葬焉，命曰会稽;
《太史公自序》上会稽，探禹穴; 《禹贡》三江既入，震泽底定. Places (new):
`zhenze` 震泽, `gusu` 姑苏, `kuaiji` 会稽山, `yuxue` 禹穴.

1. **Arrive** (`05-arrive`, 震泽). The lake has risen three years; the three
   rivers run backward. A fisherwoman: the shallows are full of great bones.
2. **Gusu** (`05-town`). The notice at 阊门: every night a giant walks toward
   会稽 and never arrives; each step lifts the water. Market (橘柚, 篠簜, 金三品).
3. **Kuaiji** (`05-hill`, 会稽山). 防风氏, bones like cart-axles, still walking
   to the assembly he came late to. Fight (root wood); riddle (人在云上 — 会);
   or the roll-call — call *防风氏，到*: he has arrived, and sits down.
   (Art alternative: 长右, 《南次二经》, 见则郡县大水.)
4. **The deeps** (`05-deep`, 禹穴). The cave 司马迁 climbed. The seal: the
   洪范's five (一曰水…五曰土), the first blank → 水 — Yu's work was water.
5. **The cauldron** (`05-cauldron`). 扬鼎, cast with three rivers, 震泽 and
   orange trees. Peak of 化神 → 炼虚 (*化神未满*). Yinyue (to the giant, earlier):
   *晚到也是到了。* At the cauldron: an old woman's face, the pattern on her hand.
6. **West** (`05-end`). The rivers run to the sea. The sixth breath lies up
   the 江, in 荆, where someone once asked what a cauldron weighs.

**Never:** the name, the night Qin came, her secret, above 炼虚.

## Chapter 6 · 荆州之鼎 (`06-jing`, gate 6 → 合体)

Heritage: 《左传·宣公三年》楚子问鼎之大小轻重焉——对曰：在德不在鼎; 《海内南经》巴蛇
食象，三岁而出其骨; 《淮南子·本经训》断修蛇于洞庭; 《中次十二经》洞庭之山，帝之二女
居之，出入必以飘风暴雨; 《禹贡》九江纳锡大龟. Places (new): `yunmeng` 云梦泽,
`ying` 郢, `junshan` 君山, `dongting` 洞庭深处.

1. **Arrive** (`06-arrive`, 云梦泽). Reeds to the sky; the storm over 洞庭 has
   not broken in eighty-one days.
2. **Ying** (`06-town`, 郢). A lord of Chu has heard someone carries cauldrons;
   his envoy asks, as 楚子 asked 王孙满: how big, how heavy? A 抉择, no gate —
   brag, refuse, or *在德不在鼎*. Market (羽毛齿革, 丹砂, 箘簵).
3. **Junshan** (`06-isle`, 君山). 巴蛇 is coiled round the island, swallowing
   the cauldron's breath as it swallowed elephants; the storm is its breath.
   Fight (root water); riddle (一张口一条尾，吞得下一头象 — 巴, 说文: 食象蛇,
   象形); or tell it the weight: *在德不在鼎* — it cannot swallow what has none.
4. **The deeps** (`06-deep`, 洞庭深处). The great turtle of the 九江 sleeps with
   the cauldron on its back. The seal: the four emblems on its shell, the south
   blank → 朱雀 (Chu honored the phoenix).
5. **The cauldron** (`06-cauldron`). 荆鼎, cast with 江, 汉, 九江, 云梦. Peak of
   炼虚 → 合体. Yinyue: *你在郢那样答，她会喜欢你的。* (the keeper's lesson)
6. **West** (`06-end`). The storm lifts. The seventh breath lies up the
   gorges, in 梁, where Yu was born.

**Never:** the name, her secret, above 合体.

## Chapter 7 · 梁州之鼎 (`07-liang`, gate 7 → 大乘)

Heritage: 《史记·六国年表》禹兴于西羌 and the 蜀 tradition 禹生石纽 (《蜀王本纪》,
《华阳国志》); 《中次九经》岷山……多夔牛; 《风俗通义》李冰斗江神 (both as oxen);
《史记·河渠书》蜀守冰凿离碓; 《抱朴子·仙药》禹步：如此三步……后有九迹; 《禹贡》梁贡
璆铁银镂. Places (new): `minjiang` 岷江, `chengdu` 成都, `lidui` 离堆, `shiniu` 石纽.

1. **Arrive** (`07-arrive`, 岷江). The 江 comes down red; the fields of 蜀 lie
   under water; a stone rhino on the bank (李冰's, set to hold the water).
2. **Chengdu** (`07-town`). The notice: the weir at 离堆 is cracking; at night
   a great ox fights the river. Market (蜀锦, 梁's silver).
3. **Lidui** (`07-weir`, 离堆). 夔牛, an ox of a thousand jin, butts the weir:
   the cauldron woke under the mountain and it thinks 李冰 is back. Fight (root
   earth); riddle (午字出了头 — 牛); or 李冰's way — *深淘滩，低作堰*: dig the
   bed, lower the weir; the water finds its way, the ox lies down.
4. **The deeps** (`07-deep`, 石纽 · 洗儿池). The pool where the infant Yu was
   washed. The seal: 禹步 cut in the stone — three steps, the prints hidden:
   how many? → 九.
5. **The cauldron** (`07-cauldron`). 梁鼎, cast with 岷, 嶓, 沱, 潜, bears.
   Peak of 合体 → 大乘. An old man calls Yu *文命*; Yinyue: *就是这个名字。
   她叫的不是我——是一个会走遍九州的人。*
6. **North** (`07-end`). The 江 runs clear. The eighth breath lies over the
   秦岭 on the plank road, in 雍 — where the cauldrons were carried when Zhou fell.

**Never:** her secret, the nine gathered, above 大乘.

## Chapter 8 · 雍州之鼎 (`08-yong`, gate 8 → 渡劫)

Heritage: 《史记·秦本纪》周民东亡，其器九鼎入秦; 《西山经》太华之山有蛇焉，名曰肥遗，
六足四翼，见则天下大旱; 《大荒东经》旱而为应龙之状，乃得大雨; 《史记·封禅书》陈宝
(若石，其光若流星，若雄鸡); 《史记·律书》. Places (new): `weishui` 渭水,
`yongcheng` 雍城, `taihua` 太华山, `chencang` 陈仓.

1. **Arrive** (`08-arrive`, 渭水). Three years' drought; the 泾 and the 渭 are
   both dust. No clear, no muddy.
2. **Yongcheng** (`08-town`, 雍城). Qin's old seat. Old men say nine carts came
   in from the east with eight cauldrons. Market (球琳琅玕). Rain wanted.
3. **Taihua** (`08-cliff`, 太华山). 肥遗 on the cliff drinks the cauldron's breath
   off the 渭. Fight (root fire); riddle (日在干上 — 旱); or the old rain rite —
   shape a dragon of mud (应龙之状); the rain comes, it flies west.
4. **The deeps** (`08-deep`, 陈仓). Rain fills the pool under the 陈宝 shrine;
   the stone crows at night. The seal: twelve pitch-pipes, the first missing
   → 黄钟 (Qin set one measure for all).
5. **The cauldron** (`08-cauldron`). 雍鼎, cast with 泾, 渭, 弱水, 黑水. Peak of
   大乘 → 渡劫 — the first thunders. Yinyue remembers the night: she asked to
   keep watch. Then: *有件事我没说。* (her secret)
6. **East** (`08-end`). Rain. The ninth breath lies east through 函谷, at the
   center — the 洛, where the nine once stood.

**Never:** what happens when the nine are gathered; above 渡劫.

## Chapter 9 · 豫州之鼎 · 定鼎 (`09-yu`, gate: 渡劫 → 飞升 — needs a decision)

Heritage: 《左传·宣公三年》成王定鼎于郏鄏……鼎之轻重，未可问也; 《中次三经》和山……
实惟河之九都，九水出焉，合而北流注于河；吉神泰逢司之，其状如人而虎尾，出入有光，动天地气;
《史记·老子列传》关令尹喜; 《禹贡》伊洛瀍涧既入于河. Places (new): `hangu` 函谷关,
`luoyi` 洛邑, `heshan` 和山, `luoshui` 洛水; `dingtao` 定陶 stays a side place.

1. **Arrive** (`09-arrive`, 函谷关). The pass-keeper asks for your book, as 尹喜
   asked 老子. Purple air from the east.
2. **Luoyi** (`09-town`, 洛邑). Nine empty stone footings where the nine stood;
   the 灵脉 thinned the day they left. Market. A light moves on 和山.
3. **Heshan** (`09-hill`, 和山). 泰逢 keeps the nine confluences; your eight
   cauldrons make the nine waters rise. It asks what you will do with nine.
   Fight (all five roots); riddle (九水合一 — 河); or its answer: *在德不在鼎*.
4. **The deeps** (`09-deep`, 洛水). Where the turtle brought the writing — and
   where a baby was found under a moon. The seal: the eight cauldrons in a
   ring, the center empty → 豫 (中; Ling may take 德).
5. **定鼎** (`09-cauldron`). 豫鼎 rises — 伊, 洛, 瀍, 涧. The ninth thunder;
   渡劫's peak → 飞升. The player sets the nine on the footings and the 灵脉 run
   back to every province (they keep the breath, not the bronze). Yinyue whole:
   *原来只有一轮。*
6. **After** (`09-end`). The player asks whether she stays: *你问了。那我就留下。*
   The provinces stay open; seeds keep coming.

**Open:** a "carry them" choice (the 问鼎 path) — keep, or 定鼎 only?
