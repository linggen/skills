# Personal CFO

**A private finance analyst that lives on your Mac.** Drop in your bank
statements; it builds the full picture, finds the leaks, and answers
"why?" — without your data ever leaving the machine.

## Your money, mapped

- **Drag & drop statements** — CSV or PDF, from any bank. Multiple
  accounts merge into one ledger; re-imports and overlaps deduplicate
  themselves; any import can be undone with one click.
- **The report builds itself** — spend, income, net, monthly trend,
  category breakdown, top merchants. Click any month or category to see
  the transactions behind it.
- **Transfers handled** — card payments from your checking account are
  recognized and never double-counted as spending.

## Finds what you're missing

- **Subscription radar** — every recurring charge, with price hikes
  flagged the month they happen ("Netflix went $16.49 → $18.99").
- **Worth checking** — double charges, bills that suddenly spike, free
  trials that just converted, new recurring charges. Dismiss what's fine;
  dispute what isn't.
- **Payment-pattern warnings** — a card payment that usually happens but
  hasn't shows up before it becomes a missed payment.

## Knows what's ahead

- **Safe to spend** — how much of this month is actually yours after the
  bills still coming: expected income, fixed charges not yet hit, and
  your day-to-day pace projected to month end.
- **Commitments** — mortgage, car loan, insurance, rent, property tax in
  one view: what's fixed each month and what share of income it takes.

## Makes debt make sense

- **Loan math, live** — add a balance and rate: payoff date, total
  remaining interest, and a slider that shows what an extra $100/mo
  saves.
- **Debt strategy** — all loans planned together: which to pay first,
  what rolling freed payments forward saves, and what the wrong order
  costs.
- **Renewal radar** — countdown to your mortgage term and insurance
  renewals, a "+1% rate" stress test, and the current market average
  alongside your rate.

## An assistant that earns it

- **Ask why** — "why was June higher?" gets named merchants and amounts,
  not a chart shrug.
- **One-click review** — a full financial check that posts prioritized
  insight cards: alerts first, wins included.
- **Ready-to-send drafts** — cancellation emails, dispute letters,
  insurance shop-around requests, mortgage rate-match letters. You send;
  it never acts alone.
- **Teaches with your numbers** — ask what amortization means and the
  answer uses *your* mortgage, not a textbook.
- **Remembers your goals** — set a savings goal once; every new statement
  gets checked against it.

## Private by construction

- Statements are parsed **on your Mac, in the app** — never uploaded.
- Account numbers and balances are stripped **before** the AI sees
  anything; it works only from redacted totals.
- All money math is computed locally and exactly — the AI explains
  numbers, it never invents them.
- **Read-only on money.** No bank logins, no payments, no auto-cancel.
  It analyzes and drafts; you act.

*Informational analysis of your own spending — not investment, tax, or
legal advice.*
